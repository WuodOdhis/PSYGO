## Why this exists

PsyDAO's bet is that a community can fund serious research and that the research should be legible to the community funding it.

That only works if someone does the translation. Most writing in this field is aimed at specialists who already know the vocabulary, or at believers who already accept the conclusion. The curious newcomer — the person who might fund a grant, join a working group, or stop repeating a myth — is usually left out.

These essays are written for that reader: intelligent, busy, and unwilling to be talked down to.
